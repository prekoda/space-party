      How to Play

      Controls

      Your ship always flies forward.

      → hold to rotate

      ↓ tap to shoot

      →→ double-tap to dash

      Touch: left side rotates, right side fires.

      Rules

      3 shots that reload. Get hit and your ship explodes — you eject as a pilot.

      Pilots can't shoot, but survive 6s and you get a new ship.

      Shoot or ram a pilot to eliminate them: +1 point. Last one alive: +1.

      Self-kills cost a point. First to the target wins.

      Powerups — shoot or ram the ? crates

      LaserCharge, then a beam that cuts through everything. Keep turning to sweep it.
      TripleNext 3 shots fire a spread of 3.
      Ricochet3 shots that bounce off walls. Watch yourself.
      Homing2 missiles that hunt the nearest enemy and explode.
      MinesDrop 2 proximity mines behind you. They hit anyone.
      FreezeFreezes a ship solid. Any hit then shatters it outright.
      ShieldAbsorbs one hit of anything.
      JousterSide blades for 12s: slice ships, block bullets, extra speed.
